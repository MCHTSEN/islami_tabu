Tabubu is a word-guessing game designed for wholesome fun within an Islamic context, perfect for family game nights.

It reimagines the traditional taboo game format with religious sensitivities in mind. Instead of inappropriate content, it's filled with concepts that encourage knowledge, quick thinking, and focus.

Features:
• Family-Friendly Fun: Form 2 or more teams and compete.
• Race Against Time: Describe the word within the time limit, but don't say the forbidden words!
• Islamic Content: Play with words and concepts that are appropriate and valuable.
• Track Statistics: See who guessed the most, which team was more successful, and view detailed results.
• Customizable Game: Adjust settings like round duration, word count, and difficulty to personalize your game.
• Rich Word Library: Hundreds of unique words, plus the ability to add your own.

Who is it for?
• Families
• Youth groups
• Anyone looking for Islamic games
• Community and educational organizations
