Tabubu is a fun, halal word-guessing game designed for wholesome entertainment within an Islamic context. Perfect for family game nights, youth groups, or any gathering with friends, it's the ultimate Muslim party game that blends trivia, charades, and "heads up" style fun.

Our game reimagines the traditional taboo format with religious sensitivities in mind. It's a great activity for Ramadan evenings or Eid celebrations. Instead of inappropriate content, it's filled with concepts that encourage Islamic knowledge, quick thinking, and cultural awareness. Test your trivia skills on topics central to Islam!

Features:
• Family & Group Fun: Form 2 or more teams and compete in this exciting group game.
• Race Against Time: Describe the word to your team before the timer runs out, but don't say the forbidden words!
• Rich Islamic Content: A quiz for your mind with words and concepts that are appropriate, educational, and valuable for every Muslim.
• Track Statistics: See who guessed the most words, which team was more successful, and view detailed results after each round.
• Customizable Game: Adjust settings like round duration, word count, and difficulty to personalize your game experience.
• Rich Word Library: Hundreds of unique words, plus the ability to add your own custom words for endless fun.
